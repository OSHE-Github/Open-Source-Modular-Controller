## Characterization & Testing
### Input Testing
Buttons and joysticks were tested using online gamepad testing tools. Several wiring issues and continuity problems were identified, including:
- Short on custom interposer board (corrected in revision)
- Stray wire strands causing unintended bridging → mitigated by moving to **26 AWG** wire

### Ergonomics
Feedback from Open Source Hardware Enterprise members indicated the controller is comfortable but slightly large for smaller hands.

### Hardware Continuity
Minor shorting issues were corrected and accounted for in later board versions. Shell design includes alignment features preventing mis‑insertion of modules.
